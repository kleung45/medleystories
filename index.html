The Beginner's Choice

Incense Sticks: The Most Common and Beginner-Friendly Option

Incense sticks are the most widely used form of incense and are often the first type people encounter. They consist of a thin bamboo core coated in aromatic material made from powdered woods, resins, herbs and fragrance compounds.

Once lit and gently blown out, the coated section of the stick smoulders slowly. This gradual burn is what makes incense sticks so approachable. They don't demand attention, they don't release fragrance all at once, and they are easy to control.

Most incense sticks burn for around thirty to sixty minutes, depending on their thickness and composition. During that time, the scent builds gradually in the space and often lingers for a short while after the stick has finished burning.

For everyday use, incense sticks are popular because they feel predictable and forgiving. If you forget about one for a moment, it's unlikely to overwhelm the room. This makes them well suited to daily rituals, quiet evenings, or background ambience.

For beginners especially, incense sticks provide a gentle introduction to fragrance without the intensity that some other formats can bring.
